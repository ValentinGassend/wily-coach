How did it go?

Wily is in alpha and built by one developer: what you say here decides what gets fixed and built next. A tap on a face is enough, a sentence helps even more.

In game, type !report and a few words to send a note about the drill you're on.

Help improve Wily, anonymously. Two things are on by default:

  • Your matches, anonymized: what the app extracts from each demo (positions, duels, grenades, buys, rounds). Every SteamID is replaced by a random ID used for that match only, and every name is removed. Never your demos, your voice, your settings or your profile.
  • How you use the app: which pages you open and for how long, the buttons you use and the errors, per day. No ID, no SteamID, no name, nothing you type.

Your matches grow the reference base, and the usage stats show what needs fixing. You can turn either one off now, or any time in Settings.

Drop your demos hereFACEIT's .dem.zst, a .dem, or a .zip / .gz / .bz2: Wily unpacks them and adds them to your matches.
